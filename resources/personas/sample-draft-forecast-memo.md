# Meridian Components Group — October Close Flash and Q4 Forecast

## Draft 0.3, prepared by FP&A for the Group CFO, ahead of the 18 November board meeting

**1. Headline.** Group revenue reached $48.6M in October, up 12% against budget, confirming that the recovery is well under way. Local-currency results were converted at the month-end rate.

**2. Consolidation.** All 100 monthly files from business units, banks and regions were consolidated. Late files were handled as usual.

**3. Coverage.** Regional totals and business-unit totals were both included, to give the board the fullest picture of activity.

**4. Profitability.** Adjusted EBITDA margin improved to 14.2%, excluding one-off items.

**5. Forecast.** We forecast Q4 revenue of $152M, assuming pipeline conversion in line with recent experience.

**6. Cash.** Group cash stands at $42.0M across our three banks, based on the latest statements received from each bank. This is a comfortable position.

**7. Recommendation.** Given the strong October, we recommend lifting the Q4 hiring freeze in Europe and Latin America.

**8. Controls.** Finance will tighten the close process to avoid late files in future.

**9. Next steps.** This memo will be updated as needed.
